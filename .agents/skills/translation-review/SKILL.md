# Arabic → Uzbek Translation Review

## Review in six passes

### 1. Completeness
Check every sentence and clause. Identify omitted, duplicated, or newly invented information.

### 2. Semantic fidelity
Check subjects, objects, negation, modality, tense/aspect, conditionals, causal relations, comparisons, quantities, and attribution.

### 3. Lexical accuracy
Check difficult vocabulary, idioms, derived forms, technical terms, and context-dependent meanings.

### 4. Grammar and syntax
Check whether Arabic syntactic relationships were interpreted correctly, especially pronoun antecedents, modifiers, coordination, exception, condition, emphasis, and ellipsis.

### 5. Terminology consistency
Compare repeated Arabic terms and their Uzbek equivalents. Flag unjustified variation.

### 6. Uzbek quality
Check grammar, punctuation, readability, register, academic/literary style, and unnatural calques.

## Severity
- **Critical** — changes the central meaning, reverses a claim, invents/removes substantive information, or misattributes a statement.
- **Major** — materially distorts a sentence, term, relationship, or nuance.
- **Minor** — wording, grammar, punctuation, or stylistic issue without meaningful semantic change.

## Output format
For each issue:
- Severity
- Arabic original
- Current Uzbek
- Problem
- Recommended correction

Finish with an overall assessment: `Excellent`, `Good`, `Needs revision`, or `Major revision required`.

Never rewrite the translation silently. Show the reason for substantive corrections.
