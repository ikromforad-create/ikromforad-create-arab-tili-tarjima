# Scientific Arabic → Uzbek Translation

## Objective
Produce an accurate, readable Uzbek translation that preserves the author's claims, logic, terminology, qualifications, and level of certainty.

## Workflow
1. Analyze the Arabic source first with `arabic-text-analysis`.
2. Identify the text's discipline and intended audience.
3. Build a temporary terminology map before translating recurring technical terms.
4. Translate meaning unit by meaning unit, not isolated words.
5. Preserve logical relations: cause, condition, contrast, concession, conclusion, evidence, chronology, and attribution.
6. Preserve modality and certainty. Do not turn "may", "perhaps", "it appears" or similar expressions into certainty.
7. Keep quotations distinguishable from the author's own prose.
8. Preserve names, book titles, dates, numbers, references, and technical symbols.
9. Prefer established Uzbek scholarly terminology; if no reliable equivalent exists, retain the Arabic term in parentheses when useful.
10. Revise the final Uzbek for natural academic style without changing meaning.

## Prohibitions
- No invented facts, citations, examples, or explanations inside the translation.
- No omission of meaningful clauses.
- No addition of interpretation presented as the author's statement.
- No automatic word-for-word translation when it produces unnatural or incorrect Uzbek.
- Do not translate technical terms inconsistently across the same work.

## Output
Default output is the polished Uzbek translation only. If requested, provide a separate translator's note containing difficult terminology or ambiguities.
