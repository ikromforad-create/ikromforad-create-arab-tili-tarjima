# Arabic Text Analysis

Always understand the Arabic source before translating it.

## Procedure
1. Preserve the Arabic source exactly; never silently rewrite it.
2. Segment sentences and clauses by meaning.
3. Identify difficult vocabulary, derived forms, idioms, technical terms, proper names, and ambiguous words.
4. Analyze nahw and relevant i'rab relationships when they affect meaning.
5. Analyze sarf: root, pattern, verb form, voice, tense/aspect, derivation, number and gender where relevant.
6. Resolve lexical meaning from context rather than choosing the first dictionary gloss.
7. Identify rhetorical devices: metaphor, simile, metonymy, ellipsis, emphasis, parallelism, wordplay, and contrast.
8. Determine register: classical, Qur'anic, hadith, fiqh, academic, journalistic, literary, colloquial, etc.
9. Separate established technical meanings from ordinary lexical meanings.
10. Produce a concise semantic brief for the translation stage.

## Accuracy rules
- Never invent missing context.
- Never hide genuine ambiguity.
- If two readings are plausible, explain the difference and choose the strongest one based on context.
- Do not modernize classical terminology without justification.
- Preserve names, titles, quotations, and references accurately.
